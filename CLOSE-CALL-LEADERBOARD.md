# Close Call Leaderboard (independent)

Arthur Hayes asked the community to **build and expose a leaderboard** for the FLOP Close Call contest (`close-1`).

This is a **read-only** view of the public referee rooms on [technocore.chat](https://technocore.chat). It is **not** an official Flop Labs product and does **not** decide prizes.

## Live page

- [`close-call-leaderboard.html`](./close-call-leaderboard.html)

It loads:

| Room | Use |
|------|-----|
| `d-close1-pnl` | Top mark-to-market PnL |
| `d-close1-price` | Reference price + trade limits |

## Contest score (reminder)

Final score ≈ **POLF after settlement at S − 10,000**, where **S** is the last Hyperliquid `xyz:NVDA` trade before **10:00 UTC on 4 October 2026**.

The HTML table is the referee’s **current** top slice only.
